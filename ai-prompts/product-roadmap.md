# Prompt: Product Roadmap

> Use when building or reviewing a product roadmap with AI assistance.

---

## Task

Help build or refine the product roadmap for: `[PROJECT_NAME]`

**Current stage:** `[idea / MVP / beta / production]`
**Time horizon:** `[3 months / 6 months / 12 months]`
**Key constraint:** `[e.g., solo builder / small team / limited budget / time to hackathon deadline]`

## Context to Provide

- What problem does this product solve?
- Who are the primary users?
- What has already been built?
- What is the desired outcome at the end of the time horizon?
- What are the biggest uncertainties or risks?

## What I Need

1. **Phase breakdown** — clear phases with goals and exit criteria
2. **Feature prioritization** — MoSCoW (Must / Should / Could / Won't)
3. **Risk register** — top 5 risks and mitigations
4. **Dependency map** — what blocks what?
5. **Success metrics** — how will we know each phase is done?

## Rules

- Be realistic. Do not inflate the roadmap.
- Flag scope that is too large for the stated constraint.
- If solo builder: suggest what to defer aggressively.
- If startup: align roadmap to fundraising or revenue milestones.

## Output Format

1. **Phase 0–3 milestone table** (phase / goal / timeline / exit criteria)
2. **Top 10 features prioritized** by MoSCoW
3. **3 biggest risks** with mitigations
4. **What to cut** if time runs short
5. **Draft ROADMAP.md** content ready to commit
